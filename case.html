Marcus HillIC5 Labs SupportToday, 8:31 AM

Confirmed. Transcripts over roughly 60 minutes exceed the context limit for your configured model provider, and v1.0 doesn’t split them yet. That’s why the record stays in Analyzing.

Workaround: split the transcript into two parts on separate conversation records for the same customer. Evidence that matches the same need will still be merged onto that need.

Fix: automatic chunking for long transcripts is scheduled for v1.1. I’ll keep this case open and update you when it’s available.
