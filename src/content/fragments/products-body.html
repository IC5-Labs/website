Why it exists

When signal, strategy, and delivery live apart,
value gets lost.

01

Signal is scattered

Customer feedback lives in tickets, surveys, calls, and notes. Nobody sees all of it, and nobody can defend a priority with it.

02

Strategy and execution are disconnected

No real-time connection between the roadmap and the work slows decisions and forces too many reviews.

03

Release is an afterthought

Customers have to pull release information and hunt for what they care about. The loop never closes.
