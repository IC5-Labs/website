The flow

One unbroken line from a spoken sentence
to a shipped feature.

1
CAPTURE

Customer conversations become evidence

Log a conversation, add the transcript, and Customer Signals Agent extracts quotes, paraphrases, and sentiment as evidence, each with a timestamp and speaker.

WHAT YOU GET
Evidence records linked to the customer, the conversation, and the exact moment it was said.
2
SYNTHESIZE

Evidence clusters into customer needs

Related evidence groups into needs and personas. New evidence that matches an existing need strengthens it instead of creating a duplicate.

WHAT YOU GET
A deduplicated list of needs, each with a count of customers and quotes behind it.
3
PRIORITIZE AND PLAN

Needs shape a live roadmap

Signals Priority Agent weighs needs by who is asking. Product managers place features on a roadmap tied to leadership initiatives and to the work itself.

WHAT YOU GET
A real-time, cross-product roadmap where every item traces back to customer evidence.
4
DEFINE AND BUILD

Features become requirements, then code

Requirements Generation Agent drafts requirements and acceptance criteria. Build Orchestration Agent hands approved work to ServiceNow Build Agent and supervises it.

WHAT YOU GET
Builders see the customer’s own words next to the requirement they are building.
5
RELEASE AND CLOSE THE LOOP

Releases tell customers what they asked for

Phase gates keep quality high. Release Notes Agent writes notes from linked evidence and identifies exactly which customers and stakeholders to tell.

WHAT YOU GET
Customers hear that their request shipped, in terms they recognize.
