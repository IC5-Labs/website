Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run it?

Australia Patch 5+ or Zurich Patch 12+, ServiceNow Otto for Platform 12.2.0+, a licensed ServiceNow Otto product, and an approved LLM provider.

Does IC5 Labs host anything?

No. The apps run in your instance as scoped applications, with no MID Server, no inbound endpoint, and no IC5 Labs-hosted component.

Which models can I use?

Any provider your administrator has approved for Now Assist. We validate against Azure OpenAI, AWS Claude, and Google Gemini.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Install with demo data on a sub-production instance, or bring a transcript to a demo and see the results live.

Can I start small and upgrade?

Yes. Most teams start on Team with Customer Signals and move to Pro or Max as they add roadmaps and release management.

Is pricing per user?

No. Pricing is per instance per year, with a user limit for each plan.
