How we govern AI
Featured · Customer Signals Agent

Transcript in. Evidence-backed needs out.

Attach a meeting transcript and the agent reads the whole conversation, not just the parts someone happened to write down. If a need already exists, new evidence strengthens it instead of creating a duplicate.

  • Transcript to structured signal. Candidate needs, pain points, feature opportunities, and personas, automatically.
  • Traceability through build and release. Evidence keeps its extraction run, prompt version, model provider, and source.
  • Runs in your instance. No MID Server, no inbound endpoint, no IC5 Labs-hosted component.
